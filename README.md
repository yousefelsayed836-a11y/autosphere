# Autosphere

A front-end automotive parts demo. Open `index.html` or serve this folder with a static server. Products, prices and cart are illustrative; there is no checkout or backend.

The vehicle cutouts, logo and hero artwork were generated for this demo. The images are approximations, not product or fitment references. The 3D viewers load only on demand and need internet access. Embedded models: [2015 Nissan Versa by Ddiaz Design](https://sketchfab.com/models/18af87c9490e4acb80a46b70ca8d86ed) as a Sunny reference; [2013 BMW 335i Gran Turismo by Ddiaz Design](https://sketchfab.com/models/e4f91073e4374de5a3ff0858f25400d2) as a 320i reference; [Mitsubishi Lancer GT by konige3](https://sketchfab.com/models/aad96b03315340dfa72a3078f4e8ddfc). Their viewer backgrounds and availability are controlled by Sketchfab and the model owners. Check model rights before commercial use.
